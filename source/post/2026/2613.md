  - $\mathrm{RMS}(x) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^2 + \epsilon}, \qquad \mathrm{RMSNorm}(x) = g \odot \frac{x}{\mathrm{RMS}(x)}$
